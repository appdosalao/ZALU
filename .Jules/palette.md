This causes severe accessibility issues for screen reader users and poor UX for desktop users who expect tooltips on icon-only actions.\n**Action:** Add `aria-label` and `title` (in Portuguese) to all icon-only buttons globally to ensure accessibility and consistent tooltip behavior.
